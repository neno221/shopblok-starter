## 0. Before M3 starts: what I need from you

1. **Nothing blocks T42.** The defaults in §1 stand unless you change one.
2. **D57, the template variants.** SPEC §9.5 gives the templates 34 variants (gallery × buy box 6, quick view 3, filter sidebar 7, cart 7, checkout 5, receipt 6) and says they belong to "M3 and M5". The default below builds every template **once** in M3, as the page the shop ships, and leaves the variants to M5, where the sections and Payload's pickers that choose them are built. If you want some or all of them in M3, say which: each is a DS entry with its baselines, and the `design-system` job grows with them (§1, D57). **Decided 2026-10-25: the default stands; the variants go to M5.**
3. **D59, listing pages without JavaScript.** Under ADR 0004 a listing page cannot read its query on the server, so the default pages by path (`…/page/2`, in the static shell) and filters and sorts in the browser; a visitor without scripts who submits the filter form lands on the search page, which renders every filter on the server. The alternative, in the row, is to drop `ensureStatic` on the listing pages alone and render every filtered page on the server, at the price ADR 0004's B2 measured.
4. **G7, branch protection and secret scanning (optional, GitHub, about five minutes).** The repository is public since 2026-10-22, so both are available at no cost (§4a). Nothing in M3 needs them.
5. **W5 at the end, on your PC** (§4a): the Windows check, your design review of the 12 templates and of the shop itself in four locales, and one keyless purchase in Cyprus and one in the US made by hand.

No keys, no accounts and no new domains: everything M3 builds runs on the compose stack, Medusa's system payment provider and the local notification provider, and on 2026-10-25 the cloud reached the npm registry and the installed packages (the dtc-starter reference is in `/opt/dtc-starter`).
